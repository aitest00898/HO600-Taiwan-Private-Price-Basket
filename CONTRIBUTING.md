# Contributing

HO600 is evidence-first research. Contributions are welcome as candidate leads, historical sources, specification lineage, or validation improvements.

Before proposing an Exact admission, provide:

- economic concept identity;
- Exact-2020 normal/list/base-price source;
- Exact-2026 normal/list/base-price source;
- dated URLs;
- specification and channel comparability notes;
- promotion exclusion check;
- concentration-surface check.

Do not add observed price movement to `Candidate_Pool` or use price change as a selection criterion.
