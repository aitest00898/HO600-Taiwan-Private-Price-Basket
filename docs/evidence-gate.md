# Evidence Gate

A concept may be admitted as Exact only when all of the following are satisfied:

- Baseline: Exact-2020.
- Current: Exact-2026.
- Price: normal / list / base / original normal price.
- Identity: product or service is identifiable.
- Specification: comparable.
- Channel: comparable.
- Geography / branch: comparable where relevant.
- Source: traceable URL.
- Dating: year/date verifiable.
- Promotion: effective promotional price is excluded.

## Allowed price semantics

Examples: 定價、原價、一般價、normal price、list price、base fare.

## Disallowed core-price semantics

Examples: 特價、優惠價、coupon-effective price、member-effective price、BOGO average, multi-buy average, bundle-derived price, delivery-platform markup.

## Comparability

Comparability A is preferred. A weaker linkage may be admitted only when identity/specification lineage is explicit enough to support a defensible direct comparison.

When SKU generation, formulation, package generation, branch, region, tariff definition, or service scope cannot be reconciled, the concept remains HOLD rather than being force-matched.
