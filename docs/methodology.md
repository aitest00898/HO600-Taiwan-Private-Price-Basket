# Methodology

## Sampling grain

The sampling grain is an **economic concept**, not a retailer quote. Multiple retailer quotes may support one economic concept.

The original TPI migration consolidated 500 observation rows into 365 economic concepts. HO600 targets exactly 600 economic concepts.

## Historical observability

Every final concept requires a direct Exact-2020 and Exact-2026 comparison. Interpolation, backcasting, nearest-year substitution, and inferred historical price are not admissible as the core pair.

## Structural balance

HO600 is deliberately not a convenience sample of whichever categories have the easiest historical evidence. Existing valid legacy pairs are preserved, but overrepresented brands, sources, product families, and subcategories are frozen for new additions while remaining slots are used to improve balance.

## Final index state

The basket remains unsealed until 600/600 concepts pass structural and evidence review. Headline calculations remain locked before sealing. Planned final views include item-equal-weight and stratified calculations; expenditure weighting remains R&D-only unless compatible private expenditure data becomes available.
