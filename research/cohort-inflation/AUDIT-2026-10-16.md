**Scope**: Research-only scaffolding, not the final index, not official expenditure weights. Source: 2026-10-16 live GitHub `main` CSV snapshots.

- Exactly **50** official HO600 subcategories mapped; **418** canonical candidates assigned to subcategory-level exposure tags (not individually observed purchase behavior).

## Critical structural blind spots

The current HO600 50-subcategory private-price basket **does not represent** full households' housing services (rent / owner-occupied imputed shelter), public utilities/electricity/gas/water, public/insured medical spending and long-term care, and important public transport/tariffs. These are integral to household expenditure, particularly retired households. An estimate formed by simply normalizing the existing mapped 50 subcategories to 100% of spend would systematically underrepresent these missing surfaces.

**Scope resolution**: publish two distinct analytical products **only after evidence and coverage gates**: (1) HO600 private-expenditure comparable subset, clearly labelled incomplete; (2) household all-spend lens incorporating external official category series under an explicit non-overlap, weighting and normalization audit. Neither is available yet. Government-set tariffs are not admitted into the original HO600 basket.
