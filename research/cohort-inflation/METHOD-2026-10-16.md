# HO600-WORK / HO600-RETIRE — Cohort Cost-of-Living & Experienced-Inflation Research Plan

**Initiated:** 2026-10-16. **Stage:** PROPOSAL + EXPOSURE AUDIT, not final research PASS. Owner: HO600 research. Work confined to `research/batch-09`. No canonical workbook or status update. No GitHub Actions.

## 1. Goal and invariants

Develop **working-household (WORK)** and **retired-household (RETIRE)** measures for the **2020-to-2026 cumulative change in the cost of a comparable consumption basket**. Distinguish (A) objectively measured expenditure-weighted living-cost changes, (B) price-change exposure by frequency of encounter, and (C) independently elicited *subjective perceptions*. Do not call A or B a psychological experience without survey validation.

Do not relax HO600 core rules: 600 private-set normal/list/base prices; identified matched economic concepts Exact-2020↔Exact-2026; seven-sheet workbook intact; no selection based on observed price changes; main = **181/600** prequalified, Basket NOT SEALED, Index LOCKED. Cohort results are **derived research outputs** and cannot redefine or seal core HO600.

## 2. Operational cohorts (predefined, not imputed from age alone)

- **WORK**: Taiwan private households with one or more principal income earners currently working as employees; primary household working-age consumption proxy, stratified by household size, urban/rural area, dependents, owner/renter, car/scooter ownership, and work commute. Do not equate every adult 20–64 with employed; working retirees and mixed households require a published classification rule.
- **RETIRE**: Taiwan households whose economic household head / principal living-cost decision-maker has withdrawn from primary paid employment and sustains consumption predominantly from pension, transfers and/or assets. Age alone is insufficient. Mixed working/retired homes analysed separately or weighted transparently; do not auto-label all 65+ as retired.
- **Official elderly comparator**: DGBAS high-age CPI cohort = every household member aged **65+**; published monthly from Jan 2025 with index back series to 2018. This is **not synonymous with RETIRE**.
- Reference households and equivalisation: publish both per-household and equivalised-person lens where source supports household size; otherwise report limits. Household geography must be aligned to DGBAS CPI coverage (Taiwan excluding Kinmen/Matsu) before direct comparisons.

## 3. Three complementary measurements and strict labels

**A. Private-price observed comparable subset — HO600-WORK-PRIVATE / RETIRE-PRIVATE:** use *sealed* private normal-price ratio p_i,2026 / p_i,2020, weighted by independently measured baseline cohort expenditure. A fixed 2020 consumption vector is preferred for strict 2020→2026 constant-basket comparability; 2025 weights may only be an explicitly labelled **2025-composition sensitivity**, not silently retro-fitted as 2020 weights. A transparent within-cell aggregation uses price relatives with fixed initial-period spend shares. 

**B. Whole-household cost of living — WORK-ALLSPEND / RETIRE-ALLSPEND:** must include major expenditure surfaces absent from private HO600 (housing services, utilities, medical care, social-care costs and other public or administered prices) through auditable official external series, with non-overlapping COICOP-like category reconciliation. The external public/official-price lens is a *separate complementary computation* and is NOT counted toward private HO600 600 items. An incomplete all-spend mapping is flagged UNPUBLISHABLE rather than normalizing the observed subset to 100%.

**C. Frequently encountered prices / perception module — WORK-CONTACT / RETIRE-CONTACT:** first validate interview/diary/official purchase frequency to set encounter weights. Keep salience and any respondent-recalled increase as separately labelled survey statistics, never substitute editorial or anecdotal frequency for money weights. Where only heuristic HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW/CONDITIONAL tags exist, output structural audit only, **no rate**.

The research data files `exposure-map-v0.csv` and `candidate-exposure-v0.csv` are **hypotheses**, not measured weights.

## 4. Prices, indices and quality tests

For cohort g with baseline expenditure proportions w_(g,i,2020) summing to 1 across all **included and fully observed** consumption cells, a fixed-base research relative is:

  I_(g,2026)/I_(g,2020) = sum_i [w_(g,i,2020) * (p_i,2026/p_i,2020)]

and cumulative change = (index relative - 1) * 100%. This is a Laspeyres-style fixed-base cost comparison. In the absence of matched 2020 weights, mark the result sensitivity/proxy, not empirical historical cohort CPI. Price relatives must share tax/pack size/unit/service-channel/geography lineage. Item count is NOT a spending weight. If a subgroup is only partially covered, denominator and price-relative coverage cannot be concealed through silent renormalisation.

Produce decomposition by 7 HO600 domains, 50 subcategories, largest weighted contributors and separately tracked missing-category share. For within-subcategory product competition, freeze pre-price-movement item allocation methodology and independently run equal-item and geometric sensitivity; these are not primary household spending weights.

## 8. Anti-misuse and interpretation

- **No formal inflation rate exists** for these cohorts from HO600 today; current original Basket=0 sealed. The words 'current-only' or 'research ADMISSIBLE' cannot be used as denominator-normalized weights.
- Selection/blindness: choose and freeze consumer cohort exposures **before** viewing candidate price movements, do not copy price or %change fields into exposure CSVs.
- Subjective 'experienced inflation' is a psychological concept; objective price pressure is best labelled 'cohort basket price change' unless a separate survey validates perceived change.
- Rent vs mortgage payment, imputed rent, interest, premiums, taxes, government services, out-of-pocket healthcare and pension entitlements need distinct accounting treatment; never silently classify asset repayment or social transfers as consumption CPI.
- Cohort population caveats: mixed pension-and-wage household, multi-generation retirees, owner-occupancy, regional differences, special diets and mobility limitations must be disclosed rather than averaged away without evidence.
